Why it matters

The misinformation challenge

Rapid sharing

Online claims can spread widely before readers have time to inspect their origin or supporting evidence.

Context matters

Keywords alone cannot establish truth. Claims require context, credible sources and careful interpretation.

Evidence first

AIMMS combines available public evidence with AI-assisted analysis to help users make more informed judgements.

Research scope

Prototype capabilities and limitations

Available capabilities

Responsive interface, claim submission, live news retrieval, published fact-check retrieval and Gemini-powered evidence assessment.

Assessment limitations

AI can make mistakes. News coverage is not proof, and missing fact-check records do not establish that a claim is true or false.

Future development

Model evaluation, verified Nigerian-language datasets, source credibility features, moderator review tools and further machine-learning development.

AIMMS is an academic prototype. Its performance requires systematic testing against labelled claims and independently verified evidence.
