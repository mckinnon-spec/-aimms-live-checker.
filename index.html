AIMMS is an academic prototype designed to help users examine online claims by bringing together recent news coverage and published fact-check reviews.

AIMMS retrieves evidence for human review. It does not automatically determine whether every claim is true or false.

Why it matters

The misinformation challenge

Rapid sharing

Online claims can spread widely before readers have time to inspect their origin or supporting evidence.

Context matters

Keywords alone cannot establish truth. Claims require context, credible sources and careful interpretation.

Evidence first

AIMMS presents related coverage and published reviews so users can inspect evidence rather than rely on a blind label.

Research scope

Prototype capabilities and limitations

Available now

Responsive interface, claim submission, recent news retrieval and display of published fact-check reviews when the API is configured.

Not a verdict engine

A news mention is not proof. No matching fact-check does not mean a claim is true or false. Sources must be checked individually.

Future development

A trained and evaluated BiLSTM model, verified Nigerian-language datasets, source credibility features and moderator review tools remain future work.
